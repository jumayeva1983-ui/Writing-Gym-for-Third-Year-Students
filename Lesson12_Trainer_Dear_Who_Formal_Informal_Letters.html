The rule: start and finish on the same train

FORMAL
no name+ Dear Sir or Madam,… Yours faithfully,
FORMAL
name known+ Dear Mr Rashidov,… Yours sincerely,
SEMI-FORMAL
teacher, colleague+ Dear Ms Karimova,… Kind regards, / Best regards,
INFORMAL
friend, family+ Hi Aziz, / Dear Aziz,… Best wishes, / See you soon, / Love,

Every letter: Hello – Why – What – Next – Bye · each What paragraph: Point – Details – Reaction/Request

WRAP detective

~5 min

Task. You bought a pair of headphones from an online shop. They stopped working after a week, and the shop has not answered your email. Write a letter to the customer service manager. In your letter:

  • explain the problem;
  • say what you have already done;
  • say what you want the shop to do.

Magic switch

~6 min

Same message, new voice. Change informal into formal.

Three tricks:
1. Full forms: I'm → I am
2. Polite frames: I would be grateful if you could …
3. Formal words: get → receive, ask → enquire, sorry → apologise

1. I'm writing to say sorry.

→I am writing to apologise.

(example)

2. Can you send me more info?

→

3. I want my money back.

→

4. Thanks a lot for your help!

→

5. I didn't get your email.

→

And now back the other way: formal into informal (a letter to a friend).

Letter corner

~20 min

Your turn. Write the headphones letter (Exercise 3) with Hello – Why – What – Next – Bye. Use P-D-R in the What paragraphs. Target: 180–240 words.

HHello + Why

Dear …………, I am writing to complain about …………, which I ordered from your website on …………

WWhat 1: the problem (P-D-R)

Unfortunately, …………. After only …………, ………… . This was very disappointing because …………

WWhat 2: what you have done (P-D-R)

On …………, I sent an email to ………… . However, I have not yet received …………

NNext + Bye

I would therefore be grateful if you could ………… . I look forward to hearing from you. Yours …………, (full name)

Word count: 0 words

Bonus (informal): tell your friend Aziz what happened in three chatty sentences.
