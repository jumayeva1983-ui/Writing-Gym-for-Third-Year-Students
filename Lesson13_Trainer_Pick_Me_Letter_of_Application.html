Today's one job: prove — with real examples — that you are the person the advert is looking for.

Look!

~2 min

1. You see an advert.

2. You write A-P-P-L-Y.

3. They invite you. A good letter = an interview.

The rule: the APPLY train

AAdvert+ PProfile+ PProof+ LLink+ YNext step

A I am writing to apply for the position of … advertised on …

P I am currently a second-year student of … I hold …

P Last summer I … , where I was responsible for … This taught me …

L I am particularly interested in this position because …

Y I am available … Please find attached my CV. I look forward to hearing from you.

Advert detective (3R)

~7 min

Read the advert three times: Role – Requirements – Response.

CITY HISTORY MUSEUM

Summer Guides wanted · weekends, June–August

We need friendly guides to show international visitors around our museum.

You must:

  • speak clear, confident English
  • be interested in history
  • feel comfortable speaking to groups of 10–20 people
  • be available at weekends from June to August

Knowledge of a third language would be an advantage.

Send a letter of application and your CV to the Museum Manager, Mr Robert Hale, by 20 May.

a) Is each requirement E (essential) or D (desirable)?

clear, confident English/
interested in history/
speaking to groups of 10–20 people/
available at weekends, June–August/
a third language/

Show, don't tell

~8 min

Each card gives a claim and a picture clue. Write C + E + R: the claim, real evidence, and why it helps at the museum.

Example: I am well-organised (C): I plan the weekly study timetable for my group of thirty students (E). This would help me to keep tours on time (R).

Red pen time

~6 min

You are the museum manager. Find the problem and write the sentence correctly. Careful: one sentence is already correct!

Order it!

~4 min

Malika's paragraphs are mixed up. Number them 1–5 in APPLY order.

I am available every weekend from June to August and could attend an interview at any time. Please find enclosed my CV. I look forward to hearing from you.

I am writing to apply for the position of Summer Guide, which was advertised on the museum's website last week.

Last year I gave a twenty-minute presentation on the history of Navoiy at a student conference, so I feel comfortable speaking to groups. I also help foreign guests at my family's guesthouse every summer.

I am a second-year student of English philology, and I hold a B2 certificate in English. I also speak Uzbek and Russian fluently.

I am particularly interested in this job because I would like to share the history of my region with visitors from other countries.

3. Show, don't tell (models)

  1. I am comfortable speaking to groups: last spring I gave a presentation to about fifty students at a student conference. This would help me to lead tours confidently.
  2. I have a real interest in history: this year I completed a project on the history of my town. I would enjoy sharing such stories with visitors.
  3. I speak Uzbek and Russian fluently and hold a B2 certificate in English, so I could help visitors from different countries.
  4. I am friendly and patient: every summer I help guests at my family's guesthouse, which has taught me to answer the same questions politely many times.

4. Red pen

  1. Dear Mr Hale, I am writing to apply for the position of Summer Guide …
  2. Correct ✓
  3. I have two years' experience of guiding tourists. / I have been guiding tourists for two years.
  4. Keep one or two qualities and add evidence: I am hard-working: last term I … (C + E + R)
  5. I would welcome the opportunity to work at your museum.
  6. Although I have not yet worked as a guide, I have … , which has prepared me to …

5. Order it

From top to bottom: 5, 1, 3, 2, 4 (A = “I am writing…”, P = “I am a second-year…”, P = “Last year I gave…”, L = “I am particularly…”, Y = “I am available…”).

6. Letter corner

Answers vary. Check: matching salutation/sign-off, all four “must” requirements with evidence, the third language mentioned if possible, availability, CV enclosed, formal register, 180–240 words.
