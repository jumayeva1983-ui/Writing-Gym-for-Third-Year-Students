Up, Down, Steady

describing data and trends

Unit 17

Today's one job: tell the reader what the numbers say: the main trend first, a few numbers next, caution last.

0

Look!

~3 min

The city opened a new tram line in January. Read the graph, then compare three texts written about it.

New tram line: average passengers per day, January–June

Number parade

In January there were 4,200 passengers a day. In February there were 4,300. In March there were 5,900. In April there were 7,800 …

Far too sure

The tram is a huge success, and it proves that everybody in the city now loves public transport!

Data commentary

The graph shows average daily passengers on the new tram line in its first six months. After a slow start, use rose sharply in spring, from 4,300 in February to 7,800 in April, and then levelled off at just under 8,000. The rise may reflect the warmer weather, although the data cannot show why people chose the tram.

Spot it: the commentary says where the data are, gives the main trend with only a few numbers, and is careful about the reason.

!

The rule: trend first, numbers second, caution last

WHERE + OVERALL→The graph shows …Overall, …
HIGHLIGHT→2–3 trends, a few numbersrose sharply from … to …
EXPLAIN→may reflect …one possible reason is …
QUALIFY→However, the data cannot show …

Four small words

by→how much it changedrose by 3,500
to→the new levelrose to 7,800
from … to→start and endfrom 4,300 to 7,800
at→a level or a pointlevelled off at about 7,800

Think in Uzbek

30 foizga oshdi → rose by 30 % (“on 30 %” emas)

ikki marta koʻp → twice as many as

6,8 soat → 6.8 hours

10 foiz punktga → by 10 percentage points

Danger zone

was risen ✗ → rose
increased on 20 % ✗ → by
in 2 times more ✗
As it is shown in … ✗
This proves that … ✗

Order the commentary

~4 min

These sentences describe the dormitory paper-recycling scheme (fictional data for practice), but they are mixed up. Choose a number (1–5): where + overall → highlight → explain → qualify.

It rose steadily from about 150 kg in January to 400 kg in May, before falling slightly to 380 kg in June.
Figure 1 shows the amount of paper collected each month by the dormitory recycling scheme from January to June.
However, the figures show only the weight collected, not how much paper residents actually used.
Overall, the amount collected grew considerably over the period.
One possible reason is that new collection boxes were placed on every floor in March.

8. Writing corner

Model (103 words): The graph shows the number of people who visited the Riverside Book Fair on each of its ten days. Overall, attendance depended strongly on the day of the week. Visitor numbers rose steadily from 300 on the first day to about 600 on Days 3 and 4, and then more than doubled at the weekend, peaking at 1,250 on Saturday. On Day 7 they fell sharply to 450 and stayed at about that level for three days, before more than doubling to 900 on Day 10. The final rise may reflect the half-price sale, although the data cannot show why individual visitors came.
