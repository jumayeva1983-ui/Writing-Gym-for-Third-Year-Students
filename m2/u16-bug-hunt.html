Bug Hunt

correcting common grammar errors

Unit 16

Today's one job: find the bug, name it, fix it, and say which rule it broke.

0

Look!

~3 min

A student wrote a short report about a weekend podcast project. Three sentences have bugs. Can you see them before you read the labels?

Agreement bug

The quality of the first episodes were poor.
Head noun: quality → was

Boundary bug

The microphone was cheap, the sound was noisy.
Comma splice → cheap, so the sound

Small-word bug

Our team bought a new equipment.
Equipment is uncountable → new equipment

Spot it: most bugs are not random. They come from one rule that the writer has not checked, so the same bug appears again and again.

!

The rule: four bug traps

AGREE→find the head nounThe list of guests for the next episodes is ready.each / the number of → singular
STOP→two full sentences + comma = bugfix: .  or  ;  or  , and / but / so  or  because / although
A / THE→countable?singular?does the reader know which one?
PREP→depend onconsist offocus ondiscuss –

Choose the repair

~4 min

Each sentence has a comma splice. Which repair is correct and keeps the meaning? Circle a, b or c.

1. The studio was booked, we recorded in the library.
a) The studio was booked, therefore we recorded in the library.
b) Because the studio was booked, we recorded in the library.
c) The studio was booked we recorded in the library.//
2. Some listeners wanted longer episodes, others preferred short ones.
a) Some listeners wanted longer episodes, while others preferred short ones.
b) Some listeners wanted longer episodes, others, preferred short ones.
c) Some listeners wanted longer episodes. Because others preferred short ones.//
3. The guest was late, the interview started at 18:30.
a) The guest was late, however the interview started at 18:30.
b) The guest was late, and, the interview started at 18:30.
c) The guest was late, so the interview started at 18:30.//
4. We had no money for music, we used free tracks.
a) We had no money for music; therefore, we used free tracks.
b) We had no money for music, therefore, we used free tracks.
c) Having no money for music. We used free tracks.//
