From Sentence to Essay

Yes, but…

although & despite

Lesson 1

Today's one job: join two opposite ideas in one sentence. Strong essays do this all the time.

0

Look!

~3 min

It was raining.

She went jogging.

Although it was raining, she went jogging.

!

The rule: two trains

ALTHOUGH + subject + verb
(a full sentence) although it was raining
DESPITE + noun or verb-ing
(no sentence!) despite the rain · despite being tired

Think in Uzbek

Although = garchi … boʻlsa-da
Yomgʻir yogʻayotgan boʻlsa-da, … (gap kerak)

Despite = … ga qaramay
Yomgʻirga qaramay, … (ot kerak)

Danger zone

despite of  ✗ never!
Although she was tired, but she… ✗
English needs only one “yes, but” word.

Magic switch

~6 min

Same meaning, new word. Change although into despite.

Two tricks:
1. Adjective → noun: noisy → the noise
2. Or use being + adjective: being young

1. Although it was noisy, I could sleep.

→Despite the noise, I could sleep.

(example)

2. Although she is young, she is very wise.

Essay corner

~8 min

Look how a writer shows both sides in one sentence:
Although some people think phones distract students, I believe they can be useful learning tools.

Their idea first, then your idea. One sentence, two sides.

Your turn. Topic: Should university students use phones in class?
