How to use the gym

  1. Open the trainer for the unit you are studying.
  2. Your answers are saved automatically, only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  3. Press Print / save as PDF to send your work to your teacher or to add it to your portfolio.
  4. A tick on this page shows the trainers you have already started on this device.
