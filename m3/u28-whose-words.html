Look!

~3 min

A student read this passage about phones and sleep. Then three classmates used it in their essays.

Source (Rahimova, 2021, p. 9; fictional source for practice)

Students who use their phones in bed fall asleep later and report feeling less rested in the morning, even when the total time they spend in bed is the same.

Copy cat

Students who use their phones in bed fall asleep later and report feeling less rested in the morning.

Synonym swap

Learners who use mobiles in bed go to sleep later and say they feel less refreshed in the morning (Rahimova, 2021).

Own words + owner's name

Rahimova (2021) found that phone use in bed delayed sleep and left students feeling more tired, however long they actually stayed in bed.

Spot it: the first steals the words, the second hides the words under synonyms, and only the third rebuilds the idea and names its owner.

!

The rule: four questions, one direction

WHOSE WORDS?→“quotation marks”(Author, year, p. 9)
WHOSE IDEA?→a citation, even in your own words
WHOSE DATA?→only what you really foundnothing invented or changed
WHO WROTE IT?→youhelp from tools or people declared

Revise from the top down

BIG→argumentstructureevidence
MIDDLE→paragraphs: one job each
SMALL→sentenceswords
SURFACE→spellingcitations and references

Big before small

~3 min

Put the revision steps in order (1–6), from big to small.

Proofread spelling, punctuation and the reference list.
Write a reverse outline: what each paragraph says and does.
Polish sentences: clarity, hedging, word choice.
Move paragraphs that are in the wrong section.
Give each paragraph one job and a clear topic sentence.
Check that every claim has data or a source, and every source a citation.

8. Writing corner

Model (76 words): Qodirova (2020) argues that regular short breaks, for example one every 25 minutes, make it easier to concentrate during long periods of study. According to her, students who work without stopping often read the same passage several times without realising it, whereas a few minutes of walking or stretching helps them to return to their reading more alert. She adds an important condition: a break spent on social media seems to bring almost no such benefit.
