Writing Gym · Module 1 · Unit 8

Polite but Firm

complaint and request letters

Unit 8

Today's one job: ask clearly, complain calmly: facts, a deadline and a next step. No shouting, no begging.

0

Look!

~3 min

Malika left her laptop for repair three weeks ago. She writes to the service centre. Three versions: which one will get her laptop back?

Too soft: a hint

Hi, just wondering how my laptop is getting on? No rush, of course. Thanks, Malika

Too hard: an order

WHERE IS MY LAPTOP?! This is a joke. Give it back NOW or I will tell everyone how bad you are!!!

Polite but firm

Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing about my laptop (repair no. 5521), which I left with you on 2 May. Could you please return it, repaired, by 25 May? Yours faithfully, Malika Rashidova

Spot it: a reference number, a date, one clear request with a deadline, and no capitals, no !!!, no insults.

!

The rule: ask in the middle, complain in order

DIRECT→Repair it.I want …You must …= an order
INDIRECT→Could you please …?I would be grateful if you could …= the best zone for letters
HINT→It is quite cold here …No rush …= easy to ignore

The complaint train: six cars, always in this order

1 PURPOSE→I am writing to complain about …
2 FACTS→what, when, where, reference number
3 IMPACT→As a result, … (facts, not feelings)
4 ACTION TAKEN→I reported the problem on …
5 RESOLUTION→I would be grateful if you could … by …
6 NEXT STEP→If …, I will have no option but to …

Think in Uzbek

Talab qilaman → I demand → I would ask you to …

Zudlik bilan → urgently → by 25 May (aniq sana yozing)

Rahm-shafqatga emas, faktlarga tayaning: sana, raqam, hujjat.

Board the complaint train

~5 min

The six paragraphs of this complaint are mixed up. Choose a number (1–6) for each paragraph. Use the complaint train.

Situation. Aziz's sports centre took his monthly fee from his bank card twice.

As a result, I was unable to pay my dormitory fee on time and had to pay a late charge.
If the refund has not been made by that date, I will have no option but to ask my bank to cancel the second payment.
I am writing to complain about a double payment for my October membership at Fit Zone Sports Centre.
I would therefore be grateful if you could refund the extra 250,000 soums by 25 October.
On 1 October, the monthly fee of 250,000 soums was taken from my card twice, as the attached bank statement shows.
I reported the error at your reception on 3 October and was told that the money would be returned within five days. It has not been returned.

Red pen time: cool it down

~6 min

Malika is angry, and her draft shows it. Cool each line down: keep the facts, lose the anger. Careful: one line is already correct!

aWHY has nobody answered my emails?!
