Photo-sharing app

#EnglishClubFilmNight

Professional network

This term the English Club is starting monthly film screenings with English subtitles, a simple way to practise listening outside class. The first screening is on 18 February. Colleagues are welcome to recommend films.

Spot it: the facts stay the same; the length, voice, emojis and call to action change.

!

The rule: build a post in four parts

FIRST LINE
do the work here→the news or a clear promise: what is new, why it matters to me
DETAILS
short→only what the reader needs: when, where, how much
ACTION
call to action→what to do next: register, reply, share, come
#TAGS
if the platform uses them→few, specific, CamelCase: #EnglishClubFilmNight

Write for everyone

ALT TEXT+describe what matters in the image, in one or two sentences
CHECK+facts, dates and names; permission for photos; credit for images; no personal data

(“Dear subscribers” deyarli ishlatilmaydi)
