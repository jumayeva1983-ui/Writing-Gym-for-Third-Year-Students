Five Stars?

writing product and service reviews

Unit 5

Today's one job: judge against clear criteria, prove every judgement with evidence, and tell readers who it suits.

0

Look!

~3 min

You want to hire a bike for a day in the city. Three people reviewed Green Wheel bike hire. Which review helps you decide?

★★★★★

Super!!! Best bikes in the world!!! Everybody must try!!!

★☆☆☆☆

Never again. Worst service ever. The staff are rude.

★★★★☆

I hired a city bike for a full Saturday. It was clean, the gears worked and the lock was included. The only problem: just two helmets in the shop, both too big. Great for a relaxed day; bring your own helmet.

Spot it: the helpful review says how it was used, gives facts, mentions one weakness and tells you who it suits.

!

The rule: the review train

HEADLINE + STARS→the overall judgement at a glance
CONTEXT→what it is; how, when and why you used it
STRENGTHS→criterion → judgement → evidence
WEAKNESSES→criterion → judgement → evidence; be fair
VERDICT→sum up; recommend it to a specific reader

Show, don't label

Label

tells the reader what to think, but not what happened.

The waiter was rude.

Evidence

shows what happened, so the reader can judge.

Nobody took our order for 20 minutes.

Think in Uzbek

Sharh boshqa xaridorlar uchun yoziladi, rahbariyat uchun emas.

“Hammaga tavsiya qilaman!” → I would recommend it to … who …

“Xodim qoʻpol edi” → nima boʻlganini yozing.

Danger zone

the best in the world ✗
very very good ✗
Dear owners, please … ✗
I advise everybody to go!!! ✗
★★★★★ … but the food was cold ✗

Label or evidence?

~4 min

A label tells you what to think. Evidence shows you what happened.

1. The bakery is overpriced./
2. A small loaf costs twice as much as in the supermarket next door./
3. The hairdresser was unprofessional./
4. The hairdresser answered three phone calls while cutting my hair./
5. The gym is dirty./
6. The changing room floor was wet, and two showers did not work./
7. The bookshop has a great atmosphere./
8. The bookshop has armchairs, and you may read for as long as you like./

Red pen time

~6 min

A review of a sports club. Replace each label with evidence: invent realistic details. Careful: one line already gives evidence!

aThe coaches are terrible.
bThe swimming pool is open from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m., and there are lanes for beginners.
cThe changing rooms are disgusting.
dThe membership is a total rip-off.
eThe receptionist was so unfriendly.

Turn the dial

~4 min

Choose the word that fits each experience. Save the strongest words for what really deserves them.

1. The film was good, but not as good as the director's last one.//
2. The service was so bad that we left before the food arrived.//
3. The museum guide was the best I have ever had: clear, funny and expert.//
4. The room was clean and quiet, but nothing special.//
5. The course was useful, and I would take it again.//
6. The hotel did not match its photos at all, and nobody helped.//

Now rewrite one sentence, making it a little softer: use slightly, a little or rather.
