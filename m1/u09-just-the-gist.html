Look!

~3 min

Read the short report. Then compare three summaries of it.

City bike-sharing: the first three months (a fictional city report)

In April, the city launched a bike-sharing scheme with 300 bicycles at 25 stations. In the first three months, the bicycles were used for about 41,000 trips, mostly short journeys between the university area and the city centre. Users praised the low price and the simple mobile app, but many complained that stations near the university were often empty in the morning. The city council has decided to add ten stations and to move bicycles from the centre to the university area every morning.

Copy and paste

In the first three months, the bicycles were used for about 41,000 trips. Users praised the low price and the simple mobile app.

Opinion added

The bike scheme is a brilliant idea and everyone should use it. Sadly, the stupid stations near the university are always empty.

Just the gist

According to a city report, the new bike-sharing scheme was widely used in its first three months, especially by students, who liked its price and app. Because university stations ran out of bicycles in the mornings, the council will add stations and redistribute bicycles daily.

Spot it: the good summary names its source, keeps the main ideas, uses new words and says nothing about what the writer thinks.

!

The rule: three moves to the gist

DELETE→cut what the reader does not need300 bicycles at 25 stations → (cut)
GENERALISE→replace a list with one termthe low price and the simple app → its price and app
CONSTRUCT→say what the details add up to41,000 trips, mostly students → widely used, especially by students

A good summary is …

SUMMARY→main ideas onlyin your own wordsno opinionnames the sourcemakes sense alone

Campus canteen survey: summary of results (a fictional student union report)

In March, the student union asked 260 students about the university canteen. Most respondents (72 %) eat there at least three times a week, mainly because the meals are cheaper than in nearby cafés. The most popular dishes are plov, soups and fresh salads. However, students identified two serious problems. First, queues at lunchtime are long: on average, students wait 18 minutes to be served, and many said that they sometimes miss the start of afternoon classes. Second, there are few options for students on special diets, such as vegetarians. Several students also mentioned that the canteen closes too early, at 16:00, although evening classes run until 20:00. The union recommends opening a second serving point at lunchtime, adding at least two vegetarian dishes to the daily menu and extending opening hours until 19:00.
