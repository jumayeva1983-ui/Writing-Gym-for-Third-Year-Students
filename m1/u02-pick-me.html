CV and cover letter

Unit 2

Today's one job: match every requirement in the advert with evidence, and let the evidence speak for you.

0

Look!

~3 min

Aziz wants a part-time job. Same applicant, two ways to sell himself. Which line would make you read on?

The advert: Blue Domes Travel

Part-time Junior Translator. You will translate tour descriptions from Uzbek into English and answer emails from foreign tourists.

Tell: the reader yawns

I am a hardworking, responsible and communicative person with good English.

Show: the reader reads on

Answered around 15 English emails a week from guests of a family guesthouse in Bukhara.

Spot it: the second line has an action verb, a task and a number. It matches the advert.

!

The rule: three trains

The CV train (one page, newest first)

NAME→ PROFILE
2 lines: who + what you want→ EDUCATION→ EXPERIENCE→ SKILLS + LANGUAGES
CEFR levels

The achievement train (every CV line)

ACTION VERB+ TASK+ RESULT
a number if you can

Translated + 25 museum labels into English + for the city museum's new hall.

The letter train (one page)

1 THE JOB→ 2 EVIDENCE
match the advert→ 3 WHY YOU, WHY THEM→ 4 CV + INTERVIEW→ 5 THANKS

Order it!

~4 min

Here is the body of Aziz's cover letter to Ms Tosheva, but the sentences are mixed up. Put them in order: 1 to 6.

I would be glad to help Blue Domes Travel present the heritage of Uzbekistan in clear, accurate English.
I am writing to apply for the part-time position of Junior Translator advertised on your website.
Thank you for considering my application.
I have also translated 25 museum labels from Uzbek into English for our city museum.
I enclose my CV and would be glad to discuss my application at an interview.
Last summer I answered around 15 English emails a week for a family guesthouse in Bukhara, so I know what foreign visitors ask before a trip.
