Look!

~3 min

Malika wrote the first line of a notice for the English Club book swap. Three classmates left a comment. Only one comment helps her.

“There will be the organisation of a book swap by the English Club, and the bringing of two books by each visitor is the rule, so come!”

Vague

“Nice! Looks good.”

Harsh

“Your grammar is terrible. Rewrite it.”

PQS: useful

“P: “Bring two, take two” would make a great slogan. Q: Which Saturday, and where? S: Start with who does what: The English Club is organising a book swap…”

Spot it: the useful comment points to a place in the text, says what a reader needs, and offers one change.

!

The rule: P – Q – S, then three tricks

PRAISE→ one real strength, and why it works: Your … works well because …
QUESTION→ where you stumbled as a reader: I wasn't sure what … means.
SUGGEST→ one concrete change: You could … so that …

Three editing tricks

WHO DOES WHAT?+ character = subject action = verb a decision was made by us → we decided
OLD → NEW+ start with what the reader knows end with the new, important part
CUT+ due to the fact that → because return back → return keep names, dates, numbers, politeness

Who does what?

~4 min

Which version is clearer: A or B? Look for the character as subject and the action as a verb.

1. A The cancellation of the trip was due to the closure of the road.
B The trip was cancelled because the road was closed./
2. A The volunteers repaired the shelves in two hours.
B The repair of the shelves was completed by the volunteers in two hours./
3. A There was an agreement among the members to meet on Tuesdays.
B The members agreed to meet on Tuesdays./
4. A We need to choose the menu by Monday.
B A choice of the menu needs to be made by us by Monday./
5. A The café manager lowered the prices for students.
B A reduction in the prices for students was made by the café manager./
6. A Our expectation is an increase in attendance.
B We expect more people to attend./

Old before new

~4 min

Read the first sentence. Which next sentence starts with something the reader already knows? Circle a, b or c.

1. The sports hall will be closed next week.
a) Repairs to the roof are the reason for this.
b) This closure is needed because the roof is being repaired.
c) The roof, which is being repaired, is why.//
2. Every visitor to our book swap brings two books.
a) These books are then sorted on tables by genre.
b) Tables sorted by genre are where the books go.
c) Genre tables then receive the books.//
3. The café now opens at 7:30.
a) Students who have early classes asked for this.
b) This change was requested by students with early classes.
c) Early classes are what the students who asked have.//
4. We have collected 120 signatures.
a) A meeting with the dean is planned to present them.
b) Next month there is a meeting with the dean.
c) We will present these signatures to the dean next month.//
6

Cut the clutter

~6 min

Cut each sentence by at least a third. Keep every fact the reader needs.

Cut: empty openers (It is important to note that) · doubled words (return back) · long phrases (due to the fact that) · hidden actions (make a decision)
Keep: names, dates, numbers, polite frames

1. Due to the fact that it was raining, the match was postponed until a later date.

→

2. At this point in time, there are a large number of free places on the trip.

→

3. In my personal opinion, I think that the poster is very much too long.

→

4. Each and every member should return back the form by Friday.

→

5. It is important to note that the bus to the museum leaves at 8:00.

→
