Who's Reading?

introduction to real-life writing

Unit 1

Today's one job: before you write a word, ask Who? Why? Where? How? Then choose the voice that fits the reader.

0

Look!

~3 min

Aziz needs the books for his writing course. He asks three different readers. Same request, three voices.

Informal: a friend

Hey Malika! Got the reading list for Writing? Can you send it over? Thanks!!

Neutral: a lecturer

Dear Ms Yusupova, Could you please send me the reading list for the writing course? I missed the first class. Kind regards, Aziz Karimov

Formal: an organisation

Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to enquire whether Zarafshon Books stocks the titles on the attached list. I would be grateful if you could let me know the prices. Yours faithfully, Aziz Karimov

Spot it: the greeting, the sign-off, Can you / Could you please / I would be grateful if, and the exclamation marks.

!

The rule: four questions, then the voice

WHO?
audience→ A friend, a lecturer, an organisation, academic readers? Do I know the name?
WHY?
purpose→ What must the reader know or do? Ask, inform, apply, complain, persuade.
WHERE?
context→ Chat, email, letter, post, report? A phone screen or a desk? How much time?
HOW?
register→ Formal, neutral or informal? Choose it once and keep it to the end.

The register scale

FORMAL+ full forms: I am, I have not I would be grateful if you could … enquire, receive, require
NEUTRAL+ Could you …, please? Thanks for … short, clear sentences
INFORMAL+ I'm, can't, got Hi, no worries, see you ! and emoji (friends only)

Real-life writing

A real reader, a practical job: ask, apply, complain, inform. Success = the reader acts.

emails, CVs, reviews, posts

Academic writing

The academic community, a claim supported by evidence and sources. Success = the reader is convinced.

essays, abstracts, research papers

Magic switch

~6 min

Same message, new voice. Change informal into formal.

Four tricks:
1. Full forms: I'm → I am
2. Polite frames: Could you please …? / I would be grateful if you could …
3. Formal words: ask → enquire, need → require, get → receive
4. No slang, no !, no emoji

1. Can you tell me when it starts?

→Could you please tell me when the programme starts?
