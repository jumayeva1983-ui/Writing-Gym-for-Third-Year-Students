Your task

The task in brief. Find one authentic text written in English for real readers: a professional email, a short opinion piece from a news website, a product or service review, or a formal letter of at least 200 words. Then work through three parts.

(a) Analyse its writing situation and name three cohesive devices that hold it together. (b) Correct five grammar or style errors that you find in it, or rewrite one weak paragraph so that it is clearer and more formal. (c) Write a reflection of about 100 words: what makes the text effective, or ineffective?

Good places to look: the opinion and letters pages of English-language newspapers, professional posts on networking sites, and customer reviews on travel or shopping websites. Quote only the sentences you work on and give the link; you do not need to copy the whole text.

Self-assessment

~10 min

Read each criterion, then choose the band your work deserves today. The weight of each criterion is its share of the 100 points. This is only your estimate: your teacher gives the mark.

Accuracy of analysis40 %

Top band: audience, purpose, context, and register are identified correctly and backed by quotations; three different cohesive devices are named and their job is explained.

Quality of corrections or rewrite40 %

Top band: every correction is right and explained by a rule; or the rewrite is clearly better, clearer and more formal, and still says what the original meant.

Reflection20 %

Top band: about 100 words; a clear judgement supported by two or three reasons from the analysis, not just “it is good”.
