Conference Abstract and Presentation Script

independent study task 6 · from your paper to the stage

IS6

Your one job: turn your mini research paper into an abstract a stranger understands and a talk that fits into 3 minutes.

0

Your task

The task in brief. Use your mini research paper (IS5) to prepare two pieces for a student conference.

(A) Conference abstract, 200–250 words: background, aim or research question, method, key findings, and implication, followed by 3–5 keywords. A reader must understand it without seeing your paper. (B) Script for a 3-minute talk, 350–450 words, written out in full: an opening that hooks the audience and names the topic, two or three key points, each backed by evidence, and a closing that invites questions. Add speaker notes that show where you pause, change the slide, or look at the audience.

Optional bonus (+5 %): 3–5 slides to go with the talk. In the script, I, you, we, and contractions are normal; the slides stay in written style.

Read each criterion, then choose the band your work deserves today. The weight of each criterion is its share of the 100 points. This is only your estimate: your teacher gives the mark.

Abstract: accuracy, completeness, and language50 %

Top band: all five moves in 200–250 words, with the key figures; it makes sense without the paper; formal and precise; 3–5 keywords.

Top band: a hook and topic statement, two or three points with evidence, and a closing that invites questions; short spoken sentences and signposts; speaker notes in the right places; about 3 minutes.
