The rule: three trains

PAST CONTINUOUS = background
(what was happening) the wind was blowing
PAST SIMPLE = main events
(one after another) I opened the door and shouted
PAST PERFECT = had + V3
(before the story moment) someone had broken the window

Think in Uzbek

was blowing = esayotgan edi (fon)

opened = oçdim (asosiy voqea)

had broken = sindirgan edi (undan oldinroq)

Story corner

The Last Bus

underline = backgroundwavy = even earlierred = time words
Hook

I should never have trusted the last bus.

Setting

It was almost ten o'clock, and the rain was falling harder and harder. I was standing at the bus stop outside the library with a pile of books in my arms.

Problem

The last bus usually came at ten past ten. At twenty past, there was still no bus. I took out my phone to call my brother, but the screen stayed black. I had forgotten to charge it.

Climax

Suddenly, a small blue car stopped right in front of me. The window came down slowly. My heart was beating so fast that I almost dropped my books.

Resolution

"Dilnoza? Is that you?" a friendly voice asked. It was Madina, my old classmate! She had moved to Navoiy a year before, and she was driving home from work.

Ending

Twenty minutes later, I was sitting in my warm kitchen with a cup of tea. Since that night, I have never left home with an empty battery, and Madina and I meet every Friday.
